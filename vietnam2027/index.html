The lighter layer

Out and about

A loose rhythm around the hotel stays: one proper sight at a time, with room to swim, eat and change the plan.

Thu · Apr 87:00–10:00am

Marble Mountains

A cluster of limestone hills just south of Đà Nẵng, with cave temples, pagodas and viewpoints reached by steep stone stairs. Go early for cooler air; allow about two hours on site.

Plan: Grab south from TMS → climb Thủy Sơn → coffee or beach lunch on the way back.

Fri · Apr 98:15–9:20pm

Dragon Bridge fire & water show

The 666-metre dragon-shaped road bridge closes briefly while its head breathes fire, then sprays water. The current official schedule is 9pm Friday–Sunday and holidays; the actual effects last only a few minutes.

Plan: Arrive by 8:30 → watch from the east-bank dragon-head side → stand back unless getting soaked sounds fun.

Sat · Apr 1012:30–9:30pm

Hội An day trip

Make this the slow day. Start with a tailor consult only if you truly want clothes, then walk the UNESCO-listed old town as the heat fades. Stay through dinner and the lantern-lit riverfront.

1pm · tailor first fitting3pm · old town + Japanese Bridge5:30pm · cao lầu / white rose7pm · river + night market

Plan: Pre-book a return car from TMS; expect roughly 45–60 minutes each way. Skip the basket boats on this compact trip.

Sun · Apr 112:30–5:30pm

Huế Imperial City

The walled Nguyễn-dynasty palace complex is Huế’s essential sight: ceremonial gates, throne halls, temples and gardens inside the Citadel. Three hours is enough for a focused first visit.

Plan: Check in → taxi to Ngọ Môn gate → finish before the current 6pm seasonal close. Move this to Monday morning if the train arrives late.

Sun · Apr 116:00–8:30pm

Perfume River evening

Walk from Trường Tiền Bridge along the river after sunset, then make the evening about Huế food rather than another ticketed sight.

Plan: Riverside walk → bún bò Huế dinner → early night. The hotel sits by the bridge, so this is the easy finish.
